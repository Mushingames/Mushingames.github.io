Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account to use MushHub?

No. You download, install and use the free version without creating anything. You only need an email if you buy a plan, to activate it.

How do I activate my plan after paying?

In MushHub open Settings → Membership, press "I'm already a member" and type the same email you used on Ko-fi. You get a 6-digit code and you're set.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Monthly memberships are cancelled on Ko-fi. When the paid period ends, MushHub goes back to the free version and your clips and settings stay on your PC.

Who charges me and which payment methods are there?

Ko-fi processes the payment with PayPal or Stripe. MushHub never receives or stores card data.

Does it work on several computers?

Your plan activates on one PC at a time. If you switch computers, you sign in again with your email and it activates there. If you have two PCs (for example a stream PC and a gaming PC), install MushHub Companion on the second one: it connects to your MushHub and needs no extra membership.

Is there a Mac or Linux version?

For now MushHub is for 64-bit Windows 10 and 11. Mac is on the list for after launch.
