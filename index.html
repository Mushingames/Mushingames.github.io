What is MushHub for?

MushHub is a free Windows app for streamers on Twitch, Kick, YouTube and TikTok. You download and install it: no account or login is needed to use it, and this page is public. It lets you control OBS with your voice, bring the chat from every platform together, prepare and apply live stream titles, and cut clips of your best moments.

What it does

🎙️ Voice for OBS

Scenes, clips, Replay Buffer, recording and mic by saying "Assistant, …" in English or Spanish.

🤖 MushAI, local AI

Understands free-form phrases, suggests titles and rates your clips. Runs on your PC, free and private.

✂️ AI clips

Listens to your recording, checks chat and suggests the best moments with a title.

💬 Multichat

Twitch, Kick, YouTube and TikTok in one chat, with emotes, read-aloud and an OBS overlay.

📝 Titles and categories

Prepares and applies titles for Twitch, Kick and YouTube, always with a preview and your confirmation.

📱 Vertical clips

Crop gameplay and webcam for TikTok and Shorts, with automatic subtitles.

How it looks

How MushHub uses your Google account (YouTube)

Only if you connect your YouTube channel in MushHub (it's optional), the app uses Google's official authorization to: see whether you are live and how many people are watching, show and read aloud your live chat messages, and change the title of your broadcast only when you confirm it. MushHub does not upload, delete or publish videos, does not read your email, and does not share or sell your data. Data is processed on your PC. MushHub's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. You can remove access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Your data stays on your PC

Recordings, clips, chat, settings and account access are stored on your computer (access tokens encrypted by Windows). MushHub does not sell or share your data. Accounts are connected through each platform's official authorization: MushHub never sees your passwords.
