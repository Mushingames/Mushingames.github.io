A Windows app that does the boring part of your stream.

MushHub is a free Windows app for streamers on Twitch, Kick, YouTube and TikTok. You download and install it: no account is needed to use it and this page is public. It controls OBS with your voice, brings the chat from every platform together, prepares and applies your live stream titles, and finds and saves your best moments with AI that runs on your PC.

Your recordings, clips, chat and settings stay on your computer. Accounts connect through each platform's official authorization: MushHub never sees your passwords.

Say "Assistant" and let go of the keyboard.

Give it any name you like: Assistant, Jarvis or Mushi. It works in English or Spanish. MushAI understands normal sentences and runs on your PC, so it's private and never charges per question.

How MushHub uses your Google account (YouTube)

Only if you connect your YouTube channel in MushHub (it's optional), the app uses Google's official authorization to see whether you are live and how many people are watching, show and read aloud your live chat messages, and change the title of your broadcast only when you confirm it. MushHub does not upload, delete or publish videos, does not read your email, and does not share or sell your data. Data is processed on your PC. MushHub's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

You can remove access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Full privacy policy.
