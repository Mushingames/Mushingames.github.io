Copy-ready text

In one line

MushHub is a streaming assistant for Windows that controls OBS with your voice, merges the chat from Twitch, Kick, YouTube and TikTok and saves your best plays.

Short description (50 words)

MushHub is a free Windows app built for streamers. It connects OBS with Twitch, Kick, YouTube and TikTok, merges every chat into one column, sets and applies your live titles with your voice and, with AI that runs on your PC, finds and crops your best moments in vertical.

Long description

MushHub is a Windows app that does the boring part of streaming. You download and install it: no account is needed to use it. It controls OBS with your voice (scenes, mute, recording and Replay Buffer), brings together the chat from Twitch, Kick, YouTube and TikTok with their emotes and lets you reply to every platform at once. It prepares and applies your live titles and categories, always with a preview and your confirmation. MushAI, an AI that runs on your PC, listens to your recording, checks how chat reacted and suggests the best moments, which you can export in vertical with subtitles for TikTok, Reels and Shorts. Your recordings, clips and settings stay on your computer, and accounts connect through each platform's official authorization: MushHub never sees your passwords. It is independent and not affiliated with Twitch, Kick, YouTube, Google, TikTok or OBS.
